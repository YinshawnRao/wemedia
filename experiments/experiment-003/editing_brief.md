# Editing Brief

## Slug

`experiment-003`

## Working Title

华语女声 10 个副歌瞬间

## Format

- 9:16 vertical
- Approximately 4.5 minutes
- No generated voiceover
- Original source audio only
- Each singer segment should last around 27 seconds

## Structure

```text
0-4s       Title hook
4s+        10 singer segments, each around 27 seconds
final 4s   Closing question
```

## Edit Rules

- Keep the music foregrounded. Text should identify the singer and mood, not fight the chorus.
- Avoid ranking words such as “第一”“最强”.
- Use lower thirds and subtle progress indicators.
- Keep source labels visible but small.
- Use direct cuts or quick wipes; do not over-animate during the chorus.
- Preserve original clip audio. No paid TTS and no local synthetic narration.

## Main Fix From Experiment 002

Experiment 002 proved the style works, but 6-second excerpts are too short for music. This version extends each segment to around 27 seconds so the viewer can hear the chorus or a full hook phrase.
