# Visual Direction

## Style Prompt

新闻纪录片式的短视频视觉：清晰、克制、有节奏，优先服务素材本身和信息密度。画面应像一个高质量编辑台，而不是营销落地页。用强对比、少量信号色、干净字幕和清楚的信息层级，让观众快速理解每个片段为什么被引用。

## Colors

- Background: `#0E1116` deep charcoal
- Surface: `#F7F2E8` warm paper
- Primary text: `#F8FAFC` near white
- Signal: `#E94B35` red-orange for key warnings and emphasis
- Support: `#2BB3A3` teal for source labels and progress accents
- Highlight: `#F2C14E` warm yellow for timestamps and quoted numbers

## Typography

- Primary: `Inter`, `system-ui`, sans-serif
- Editorial accent: `IBM Plex Sans Condensed`, `Arial Narrow`, sans-serif
- Use tight hierarchy, but keep letter spacing at `0`.

## Motion

- Cuts should feel deliberate, not flashy.
- Use fast but readable lower thirds, timestamp reveals, source labels, and map/quote callouts.
- Prefer opacity, y, scale, and subtle blur-like visual treatment created with CSS filters only when legibility remains strong.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use generic blue-purple SaaS gradients.
- Do not hide source footage behind heavy blur or dark overlays.
- Do not stack decorative cards inside cards.
- Do not use random animation, infinite repeats, or constantly moving backgrounds.
- Do not let captions cover faces, source labels, or key visual evidence.
